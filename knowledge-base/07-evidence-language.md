# Evidence language policy

Preferred wording when describing evidence:

- "the evidence supports": published data indicates compatibility or low risk.
- "the evidence does not establish": data is absent, inconclusive or contradictory.
- "published investigation reports": a finding is attributed to published literature.
- "review/retest requested": the available evidence is insufficient to conclude.

Avoid: "100% safe", "certified safe", probabilities without a cited source, claims that every test result expires after a fixed period, and presenting synthetic fixtures as observations.
