# Oddy test scope

The Oddy test exposes silver, copper and lead coupons to the volatile compounds a material emits in a closed vessel, usually for 28 days at 60 °C. Materials are rated permanent (P), temporary (T) or unsuitable (U) from a visual inspection of the coupons.

A 2025 review lists limits of the test. It primarily assesses corrosion of silver, copper and lead, so it may not effectively assess the risk to other materials such as paper, paints or plastics. The rating rests on subjective visual inspection. The test does not identify the compounds a material emits, which is why a material can pass and still cause problems.

ArtifactGuard treats a result as a scoped observation tied to a material or revision, protocol, institution and coupon outcome, not as a universal "safe" property.
