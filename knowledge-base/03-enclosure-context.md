# Enclosure context

CCI Technical Bulletin 32 gives a simple relationship for the concentration of a pollutant released by a product into an enclosure: `C = E A / V N`, where C is the concentration in the enclosure (µg/m³), E the emission rate of the pollutant from the product (µg/m²h), A the product surface area (m²), V the enclosure volume (m³) and N the leakage rate of the enclosure (h⁻¹).

From the formula, CCI notes that the most dangerous scenario is a vulnerable object in a well-sealed enclosure with a large surface area of an emissive product. CCI's general guidelines also note that closed cabinets and display enclosures reduce risks from pollutants and incorrect humidity.

ArtifactGuard evaluates the formula only when all four inputs are supplied. It does not invent inputs and does not define a numeric safety threshold.
