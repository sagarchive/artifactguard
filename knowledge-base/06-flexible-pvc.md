# Flexible PVC

CCI Technical Bulletin 32 lists plasticizer from degraded flexible PVC as a cause of stains (discolouration or yellowing) and embrittlement in direct contact with objects, and viscous plasticizer exudate on degraded flexible PVC as a cause of compounds leaching from an object's surface. It also lists benzoic acid from degraded plasticizer from flexible PVC as an airborne pollutant that corrodes copper and bronze.

The bulletin does not name a specific plasticizer, and ArtifactGuard does not infer one. The condition of the PVC is not part of an ArtifactGuard scenario.

Source: https://www.canada.ca/en/conservation-institute/services/conservation-preservation-publications/technical-bulletins/products-used-preventive-conservation.html
