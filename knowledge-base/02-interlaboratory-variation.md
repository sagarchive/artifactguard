# Interlaboratory variation

A 2024 review and interlaboratory comparison had seven European institutions run their own 3-in-1 Oddy procedures (28 days, 60 °C) on the same ten materials, blind and in duplicate. The 3-in-1 version, 60 °C and 2 g of material are common practice, but other variables are not standardized, and differences between institutions' results were still observed. Some came from coupon preparation, either the sanding pattern or the edge area.

A 2025 study summarizes the main discrepancies as inhomogeneous coupon preparation, evaluator inexperience, and especially inconsistent evaluation of copper and lead coupons classified as temporary.
