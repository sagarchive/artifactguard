01

The problem

Glue, foam and paint in a display case can give off fumes that corrode metal objects. Labs test materials, and a "pass" sounds final. It only means the one sample they tested did not hurt the metal in their setup.

02

What this does

It looks up published test results for your material, shows which labs and batches they come from, and says where they disagree or do not apply. Every line links to its source.

03

Where Sanity fits

Each test, product batch, lab and source is a linked record in Sanity, fetched live. The links are what let it warn that a pass missed a later problem. The answer itself comes from fixed rules, not from Sanity or an AI.

04

What it will not do

It never says "safe" and never gives a percentage. It only knows what a few published papers report. It helps you think and does not replace a conservator.

Reading a result

Enclosure concentration estimator

Simplified CCI relation C = E × A / (V × N). It estimates concentration only and sets no safety threshold.

Scope

What a result does not mean

“No conflict found” means the scoped evidence did not reveal one. It is not a safety guarantee and does not replace testing or a conservator’s judgment.
